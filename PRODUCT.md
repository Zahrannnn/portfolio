# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers, and freelance clients who land on Mohamed Zahran’s portfolio from GitHub, LinkedIn, or a shared link. They are usually on a laptop, scanning quickly for credibility, stack fit, and live work. Success is a clear first impression in under a minute, then easy paths to projects and contact.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio that presents Mohamed as a frontend engineer at RICOH Europe (formerly Corelia), shipping product UIs and agentic systems. It should showcase selected projects and client sites, reinforce TypeScript / React / Next craft, and make contact effortless. Success looks like: accurate role and employer, trustworthy links, and a distinctive intro that does not feel like a generic AI template.

## Brand Personality

Joyful, precise, craft-forward. Warm playful energy (pixel joy) with senior engineering clarity. Confident without luxury theater. Voice is direct and human; no “calibrating scene” jargon.

## Anti-references

- Dark-gold SaaS night-mode preloaders (glow rings, grain vignettes, hero-metric %)
- Purple-on-black AI landing aesthetics
- Framing Corelia/RICOH work as a personal product named CORELIA
- Fake load percentages disconnected from real asset loading
- Editorial-magazine cliché (track-spaced mono labels + italic display serif + ruled columns) for this surface

## Design Principles

1. Honesty first: load state reflects real hero/3D progress; links go somewhere real.
2. One clear composition per surface; no stacked decorative chrome.
3. Motion is ease-out only; always honor prefers-reduced-motion.
4. Identity preservation for Amiamie and existing light primary page, with joyful accents on the intro.
5. Accessibility is default: status announcements, aria-busy, visible hierarchy, WCAG AA contrast where we control color.

## Accessibility

Target WCAG AA for new surfaces. Support reduced motion. Preloader must expose loading status to assistive tech (`role="status"`, `aria-busy`, live progress). Decorative layers are `aria-hidden`.
