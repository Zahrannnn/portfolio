# mzahran-projects

Use this skill to browse or select Mohamed Zahran's projects.

## Steps

1. Fetch https://mzahran.tech/agents.json for role context, and the Works
   section of https://mzahran.tech for the live gallery.
2. Present projects with: name, one-line description, tech stack, and link.
3. Match projects to the user's intent, for example:
   - "3D work" → RICOH 3D Building & Space Viewer, CyrusLearn
   - "real-time dashboards" → Tailgating Detection System, NedSwiss CRM
   - "AI integration" → Wasla Feedback Moderation API, AI Job Searcher,
     AI-Powered Translation Platform
   - "RTL/Arabic" → OZ Storefront, NedSwiss CRM
